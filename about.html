To the most beautiful soul ever to grace this earth

Mohammad Turab Ali

To the one who never left us, not even for a breath.
To the soul forever woven into our hearts.
To the one whose kindness knows neither end nor measure.
To the one who loved us, and still loves us, as no one ever did.

Here is a little offering of love — a mere speck beside the boundless love you have poured upon us — dedicated to the one who is, and shall always be, everything to us.

— Baba, Ammi & Bhayya
